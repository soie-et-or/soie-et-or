Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what personal data Soie et Or collects when you visit soie-et-or.com, why, and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The data controller is Philippine, publisher of Soie et Or, based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: hello@soie-et-or.com.

What we collect

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Analytics. We currently do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click an affiliate link, you leave our site. The affiliate network (such as Awin) and the retailer may set cookies to record that you came from Soie et Or, so that a commission can be attributed if you buy. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. We receive reports of sales and commissions, not your name or contact details.

Retention

Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your message and are deleted after at most two years. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider for a limited period.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. Email us to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
