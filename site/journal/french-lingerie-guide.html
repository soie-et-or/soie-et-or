French Lingerie: The Quiet Art of Dressing Underneath

Silk, fine lace and the right fit. Why French women treat lingerie as the first layer of a good outfit.

21 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lingerie

In France, lingerie is not reserved for special occasions. It is simply the first layer of getting dressed, chosen with the same care as a coat or a pair of shoes. Nobody else needs to see it. That is exactly the point.

Fit comes first

Beautiful lingerie that does not fit is never beautiful. Get measured properly, and measure again every year or two, because sizes change. A good fit makes everything you wear over it sit better, from a silk blouse to a fine knit.

Fabrics worth paying for

  • Silk. Soft, breathable and quietly luxurious. A silk camisole works under a blazer as well as on its own.
  • Fine lace. Look for lace that lies flat and does not show through clothes.
  • Cotton and modal. For everyday comfort, in good quality, they are just as chic.

Colours that work under everything

Build the basics in shades close to your skin tone, plus black and ivory. Then add one or two sets in a colour you love simply because it makes you feel good.

Pieces that go beyond underwear

A silk slip dress, a lace-trimmed camisole or a fine bodysuit can be worn as part of an outfit. Under an oversized blazer, or with a lace hem peeking out beneath a knit, they add softness to tailoring. It is a very Parisian contrast.

Caring for it

  • Hand wash in cool water, or use a mesh bag on a delicate cycle.
  • Never tumble dry silk or lace.
  • Store bras flat or cup-in-cup so they keep their shape.

Treated well, good lingerie lasts for years. In cost-per-wear terms it is one of the best investments in a wardrobe.
