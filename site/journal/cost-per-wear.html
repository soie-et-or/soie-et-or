Cost Per Wear: How to Think About Investment Pieces

A simple calculation that changes how you shop, and why the expensive coat can be the economical choice.

20 October 2026 · 4 min read

This article contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, Soie et Or may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. How this works.

Cost per wear is the price of a piece divided by the number of times you wear it. It is a simple idea, and it is the clearest way to judge whether a luxury purchase is indulgent or sensible.

An example

A coat that costs 900 euros and is worn 60 days a year for five years has been worn 300 times. That is 3 euros per wear. A trend coat that costs 150 euros and is worn ten times before it feels dated costs 15 euros per wear. The cheaper coat was five times more expensive.

What makes a good investment piece

  • You will wear it often. Be honest about your real life, not the life in the picture.
  • It works with what you own. If it needs new shoes and a new bag, add those to the price.
  • It is built to last. Look at seams, lining, buttons and fabric weight.
  • It will not date quickly. Classic shapes in classic colours.

Jewelry and watches

Fine jewelry and watches can be the ultimate cost-per-wear pieces. A ring worn every day for twenty years becomes almost free per wear, and good pieces can often be resold. Buying pre-owned lowers the entry price further.

When not to use it

Cost per wear is a tool, not a rule. Some pieces are worth it for one perfect evening. The idea is not to stop enjoying fashion, but to spend with intention.
