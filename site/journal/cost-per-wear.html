Cost Per Wear: How to Think About Investment Pieces

A simple calculation that changes how you shop, and why the expensive coat can be the economical choice.

20 October 2026 · 4 min read

Some links are affiliate links; we may earn a small commission. How this works

The Edit in this story
The Lowest Cost Per Wear

Cost per wear is the price of a piece divided by the number of times you wear it. It is a simple idea, and it is the clearest way to judge whether a luxury purchase is indulgent or sensible.

An example

A coat that costs 900 euros and is worn 60 days a year for five years has been worn 300 times. That is 3 euros per wear. A coat like the one in our winter capsule is exactly this kind of piece. A trend coat that costs 150 euros and is worn ten times before it feels dated costs 15 euros per wear. The cheaper coat was five times more expensive.

What makes a good investment piece

  • You will wear it often. Be honest about your real life, not the life in the picture.
  • It works with what you own. If it needs new shoes and a new bag, add those to the price. A ten-piece capsule makes this easy to check.
  • It is built to last. Look at seams, lining, buttons and fabric weight.
  • It will not date quickly. Classic shapes in classic colours. This is the idea behind quiet luxury, too.

Jewelry and watches

Fine jewelry and watches can be the ultimate cost-per-wear pieces. A ring worn every day for twenty years becomes almost free per wear, and good pieces can often be resold. Our guides to everyday gold, buying gold when it is expensive and the small watch go further. Buying pre-owned lowers the entry price further.

When not to use it

Cost per wear is a tool, not a rule. Some pieces are worth it for one perfect evening. The idea is not to stop enjoying fashion, but to spend with intention.
