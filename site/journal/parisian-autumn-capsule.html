The Parisian Autumn Capsule: Ten Pieces, Endless Outfits

A small wardrobe that does a lot of work, built the way Parisian women actually dress.

20 October 2026 · 6 min read

Parisian style has a reputation for looking effortless. The effort is real, but it goes into choosing, not into dressing. A Parisian wardrobe tends to be small, quiet in colour and very well edited. Almost everything works with almost everything else, so getting dressed takes two minutes.

This is the ten-piece capsule we would build for autumn. The palette stays narrow on purpose: navy, black, ecru, camel and one deep accent. That restraint is what lets ten pieces turn into dozens of outfits.

The ten pieces

  1. A camel coat with a clean shoulder. The single most useful thing you will own from October to March. Look for a long, straight cut that closes without strain over a knit.
  2. A slightly oversized wool blazer. Navy or black. It goes over jeans, over a slip dress, over a turtleneck. Sandro does this shape well, with sharp tailoring that never looks corporate.
  3. A cream cable or rib knit. Chunky enough to wear alone, fine enough to fit under the blazer.
  4. A fine black turtleneck. Merino or cashmere. The quiet base layer for everything else on this list.
  5. Straight dark jeans. Full length, no rips, no fading. Acne Studios is the reference for denim that looks expensive because it is plain.
  6. Wide, fluid trousers. In wool or a heavy crepe. They move when you walk, which is half of their charm.
  7. A silk or satin midi skirt. Worn with a chunky knit and flat boots, it is the most Parisian outfit on this list.
  8. A leather jacket with a little edge. IRO, founded in Paris in 2005 with roots in rock music, is known for exactly this kind of jacket: fitted, a bit worn-in in spirit, never costume.
  9. Loafers or flat ankle boots. Paris is a walking city. Heels are the exception, not the rule.
  10. One piece of everyday gold. A ring or a chain you never take off. More on that in our guide to everyday gold.

How to wear it

Three formulas cover most days. Jeans, turtleneck, blazer and loafers for the office. Silk skirt, cream knit and ankle boots for a long lunch. Wide trousers, leather jacket and a fine knit for the evening. Add the coat and you are done.

The rule that ties it together: one interesting thing per outfit. If the skirt shines, everything around it is matte. If the jacket has edge, the rest stays soft.

What to leave out

Logos, trend colours you will tire of by January, and anything that needs a specific shoe to work. A capsule only works if every piece earns its place.
