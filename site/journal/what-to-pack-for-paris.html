What to Pack for Paris: Four Looks for the Weekend

A day of walking, a long lunch, dinner out and the rainy afternoon. Four outfits, built from the same few pieces.

26 November 2026 · 4 min read

Some links are affiliate links; we may earn a small commission. How this works

The Edit in this story
Daytime in Paris

You have arrived in one of the three travel outfits. Now the case opens. For two or three days in Paris you need four looks, and they should share as many pieces as possible: the coat you travelled in, one bag, two pairs of shoes. Here is how to build them.

Daytime in Paris

Museums, markets and the long walk between them. Flat shoes, a coat that works over everything and a bag that holds a scarf, a book and the sunglasses.

The long lunch

A terrace in the Marais or a bistro on the Left Bank. Relaxed, but finished: a good knit or a silk shirt, trousers that sit well, and the one piece of jewellery you wear every day.

Dinner out

The evening is where Paris dresses up, quietly. A slip dress or a silk top, a heel you can walk on over cobblestones, and a small bag that holds only what you need.

A rainy afternoon

It will rain at least once. A trench or a hooded coat, boots that do not mind puddles and a large scarf. Rain is no reason to look less put together.

The rule

Every piece in the case should appear in at least two of the four looks. If it does not, it stays at home.
