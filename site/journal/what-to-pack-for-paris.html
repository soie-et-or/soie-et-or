What to Pack for Paris: Four Looks for the Weekend

A day of walking, a long lunch, dinner out and the afternoon it rains. Four winter outfits for a weekend in Paris, piece by piece.

You have arrived in one of the three travel outfits. Now the case opens. A winter weekend in Paris asks for four looks: a day of walking, a long lunch, dinner out and the afternoon it rains. Pack boots rather than heels, tights rather than bare legs, and one coat that can do two jobs.

Winter daytime in Paris

Museums, markets and the long walk between them. A warm coat over silk and denim, flat boots and a bag that holds a scarf, a book and the gloves you took off for coffee.

A rainy afternoon

The rule
