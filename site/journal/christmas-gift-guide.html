The Gifts We Would Wrap: Our Favourites for Christmas

Fine gold, soft cashmere and a few things for the road. The pieces we would choose for the people we love, and one or two for ourselves.

A good gift is not the most expensive thing in the shop. It is the thing someone wears every day and thinks of you. That is the only rule we used for this list: everything here is a piece we would keep ourselves, and all of it comes from our own looks and edits.

For her: one good piece

If the budget allows one serious gift, make it gold. A ring, a chain or a pair of hoops is worn every day, survives every trend and becomes almost free per wear. More on choosing well in buying gold when gold is expensive, and more pieces in our jewelry gift edit.
