What to Pack for a Weekend in Paris

One carry-on, one bag and one outfit to travel in. Three ways to arrive in Paris looking as if you live there.

21 October 2026 · 4 min read

The most elegant travellers are rarely the ones with the most luggage. They carry one good case, one bag that works from the train to dinner, and they travel in the pieces they will wear all weekend. For two or three days in Paris, that is all you need.

The rule

Pack in one palette, so everything mixes: black, grey, cream and camel, with one colour at most. Wear your bulkiest pieces on the journey, the coat and the boots, so the case stays light. And if a piece only works with one other piece, leave it at home. Everything in the bag should earn its place twice.

Three ways to arrive

Each look starts with the case and the bag, then the outfit you travel in, from the coat down to the shoes. Flat soles in every one: Paris is a walking city, and the first walk starts at the station.
