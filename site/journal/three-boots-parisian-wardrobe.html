The Three Boots Every Parisian Wardrobe Needs

A flat ankle boot, a knee-high riding boot and one with a heel. Together they cover every autumn and winter day.

Paris is a walking city, and from October to March its women walk in boots. Not dozens of pairs, just three good ones that together cover every occasion, and one editorial bonus at the end.

In this story The flat ankle boot · The riding boot · The boot with a heel · Bonus: the Western boot

1. The flat ankle boot

A Chelsea or a simple zip ankle boot in black leather or dark brown suede. It is the boot you wear four days a week: with straight jeans, wide trousers, a midi skirt. Choose a slim sole and a clean toe so it looks as good at a dinner as on the street.

2. The knee-high riding boot

Knee-high with a flat sole, in smooth leather that fits the calf well. Worn with a knee-length skirt or a sweater dress, it is the most elegant winter outfit there is. With tucked-in jeans it turns casual and very French.

3. The boot with a heel

An ankle boot or a knee-high with a block or slim heel, for evenings, offices and anything slightly dressed up. A pointed or almond toe looks sharpest, under trousers or a long skirt.

The heeled ankle boot works best almost hidden. Let straight or wide trousers fall over it so only the pointed toe shows, and the heel quietly lengthens the leg. With a midi skirt, add sheer black tights so the line runs unbroken from hem to toe. Keep the heel mid-height: high enough to change the way you stand, low enough for cobblestones and the metro.

The heeled knee-high wants a skirt or a dress that meets it, or nearly: a midi skirt, a slip dress, a long knit, with no more than a hand’s width of tights between hem and boot. Sleek leather looks sharp under a long coat; slouchy suede can be pushed down a little and feels softer, even with straight jeans tucked in. Keep everything above it simple, a roll-neck or a plain knit, and let the boot do the talking.

And for something tougher

A lace-up combat boot adds edge to a feminine outfit: a silk skirt, a soft knit, chunky boots. Miu Miu and Acne Studios both do versions that balance tough and refined.

Making them last

  • Protect new leather and suede with a spray before the first rain.
  • Use boot shapers or rolled magazines in tall boots so the shaft keeps its shape.
  • Have a cobbler add protective rubber soles; it can add years.
  • Let them dry slowly, away from the radiator.

Three pairs, looked after well, will see you through many winters. That is the Parisian way of buying shoes.

Editorial bonus: the Western boot

Not one of the three, but the pair that keeps showing up on the streets of Paris every autumn. A Western boot gives a quiet wardrobe a little attitude: wear it with a long knit, a slip dress or straight jeans, and keep everything else simple. Burgundy leather, black embroidery or an animal print make it a statement; one pair is enough.
