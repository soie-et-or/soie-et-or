The Three Boots Every Parisian Wardrobe Needs

A flat ankle boot, a knee-high riding boot and one with a heel. Together they cover every autumn and winter day.

21 October 2026 · 5 min read

Boots

Paris is a walking city, and from October to March its women walk in boots. Not dozens of pairs, just three good ones that together cover every occasion.

1. The flat ankle boot

A Chelsea or a simple zip ankle boot in black leather or dark brown suede. It is the boot you wear four days a week: with straight jeans, wide trousers, a midi skirt. Choose a slim sole and a clean toe so it looks as good at a dinner as on the street.

2. The knee-high riding boot

Flat or with a low heel, in smooth leather that fits the calf well. Worn with a knee-length skirt or a sweater dress, it is the most elegant winter outfit there is. With tucked-in jeans it turns casual and very French.

3. The boot with a heel

An ankle boot with a block or slim heel for evenings, offices and anything slightly dressed up. A pointed or almond toe looks sharpest under trousers.

And for something tougher

A lace-up combat boot adds edge to a feminine outfit: a silk skirt, a soft knit, chunky boots. Miu Miu and Acne Studios both do versions that balance tough and refined.

Making them last

  • Protect new leather and suede with a spray before the first rain.
  • Use boot shapers or rolled magazines in tall boots so the shaft keeps its shape.
  • Have a cobbler add protective rubber soles; it can add years.
  • Let them dry slowly, away from the radiator.

Three pairs, looked after well, will see you through many winters. That is the Parisian way of buying shoes.
