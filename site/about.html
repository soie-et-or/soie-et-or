About Soie et Or

Soie et Or means silk and gold. It is the shortest way we know to describe a certain kind of Parisian elegance: soft fabrics, a few precious things, and nothing that shouts.

This site is an edit of quiet luxury. We write about Parisian ready-to-wear, fine jewelry and watches, and the art of building a small wardrobe that lasts. We cover labels such as Sandro, IRO, Acne Studios, Marni and Miu Miu, and fine jewelry and watches from houses like Pomellato, Cartier and Chopard, including pre-owned pieces.

How we choose

  • We favour quality, fit and longevity over trends.
  • We recommend pieces we would wear ourselves, and we say so when something is a splurge.
  • We prefer pieces that work with what you already own.

How we are funded

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you buy through them, we may earn a commission from the retailer, at no extra cost to you. Retailers never pay to be featured and never see our articles before they are published. Read our full affiliate disclosure.

Who is behind Soie et Or

Soie et Or is curated by Stefanie, based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Every article, edit and pin on this site is chosen and written by her.
